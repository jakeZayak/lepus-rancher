LEPUS Rancher

Vacuum up Lepus, feed them carrots, and sell their eggs.

  • Right thumb drag to look around
  • Hold VAC to vacuum Lepus, carrots and eggs
  • Tap a slot to pick it, hold SHOOT to fire it out
  • Tap LAB when you're next to the Upgrade Lab

  • Pens hold your Lepus. Shoot carrots in to make eggs
  • Hopper shoot eggs in to sell them
